VitruvianGodot human body and head models, plus body and face base-color textures: https://github.com/ibrews/VitruvianGodot. The character geometry and textures are CC0; the repository code is MIT. The GLB body includes locomotion clips credited to Mixamo under Adobe's asset license; see that repository's README.md and Adobe's FAQ (https://helpx.adobe.com/creative-cloud/faq/mixamo-faq.html). Changes here: body and head are combined in a lightweight Web scene, materials and clothing are assigned in GDScript, and texture images were resized from 2048px to 1024px. These are original fan-game characters, not Rockstar or GTA VI character assets.
